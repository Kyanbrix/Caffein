# Caffein — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** September 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Caffein** ("Caffein", "the Application", "the Bot", "we", "us", or "our"), developed and maintained by **Kyanbrix**, collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Caffein through Discord.

By using, inviting, or interacting with Caffein, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Information We Collect

Caffein may collect and store information that is necessary to provide its features and functionality.

Depending on how Caffein is configured and which features are used, this may include:

### Discord Information

* Discord User ID
* Discord Server/Guild ID
* Discord Channel ID
* Discord Role ID
* Discord Message ID
* Discord username or display name when required for a feature
* Discord server and channel configuration information

Caffein generally uses Discord IDs rather than requiring users to provide personal information directly to the Application.

### Leveling Information

If the leveling system is enabled, Caffein may store information such as:

* User ID
* Server ID
* Experience points (XP)
* User level
* Voice activity used for XP calculations
* Message activity used for XP calculations
* Related leveling statistics

### Invite Tracking

If invite tracking features are enabled, Caffein may store information such as:

* Server ID
* Inviter User ID
* Invited User ID
* Invite code
* Invite-related statistics

This information is used to provide invite tracking and related server features.

### Moderation and Security Information

Caffein may process information necessary to provide moderation and security features, including:

* Message content when required for filtering or moderation
* User ID
* Channel ID
* Server ID
* Message ID
* Detected links
* Moderation-related events
* Anti-raid events

This information may be processed to detect spam, phishing links, malicious links, prohibited words, raids, or other activity configured by a server administrator.

### Anonymous Confessions

If a server enables anonymous confession functionality, Caffein may process and store the submitted confession.

Although the confession may be displayed anonymously to other server members, associated technical information may be retained for moderation, abuse prevention, security, or operational purposes.

Anonymous does not necessarily mean that no information is retained.

### AI Features

Caffein may provide AI-powered functionality.

When you interact with an AI feature, the message or information necessary to generate a response may be processed by the Application and, where applicable, third-party AI service providers.

AI-generated responses may be based on the information provided through the interaction.

Users should not submit passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to the AI features.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information collected by Caffein may be used to:

* Provide and operate the Application;
* Provide leveling and leaderboard functionality;
* Track server invites;
* Provide moderation and security features;
* Detect spam, phishing, or potentially malicious links;
* Provide anonymous confession functionality;
* Provide AI-powered features;
* Maintain server configuration;
* Diagnose bugs and technical problems;
* Prevent abuse and misuse;
* Improve the reliability and functionality of Caffein;
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use collected information for purposes unrelated to operating, maintaining, securing, or improving Caffein unless otherwise disclosed.

## 3. Message Content

Some Caffein features may require access to message content.

For example, message content may be processed when:

* A moderation filter is enabled;
* A prohibited-word filter is enabled;
* A link or phishing detector is enabled;
* An AI chatbot feature is used;
* Another feature explicitly requires message content.

Caffein does not need to permanently store every message sent in a Discord server merely because it has access to messages.

Where possible, message content should only be processed when necessary for the relevant feature.

## 4. Data Storage

Information collected by Caffein may be stored in databases or other systems used to operate the Application.

The specific information stored may vary depending on:

* Features enabled by a server administrator;
* Commands used by users;
* Application updates;
* Technical requirements;
* Security and moderation requirements.

We take reasonable measures to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no electronic storage or transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 5. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant functionality, maintain the Application, prevent abuse, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal or security requirements.

Different types of information may have different retention periods.

For example, temporary moderation or security information may be retained for a shorter period than persistent leveling or server configuration data.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized where reasonably practical.

## 6. Data Sharing

Caffein does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed when reasonably necessary to:

* Operate Caffein;
* Provide a requested feature;
* Use an external service required by a feature;
* Maintain security;
* Prevent abuse or fraud;
* Comply with legal obligations;
* Respond to valid legal requests.

If a feature uses a third-party service, information required for that feature may be transmitted to that service.

Third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

## 7. Third-Party Services

Caffein operates through Discord and may use third-party services for certain functionality.

These services may process information according to their own policies.

Caffein is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, availability, or policies of third-party services.

Your use of Discord remains subject to Discord's own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

## 8. Server Administrators

Server administrators control whether many Caffein features are enabled on their server.

Administrators may configure, disable, or remove features depending on the functionality provided by Caffein.

Administrators are responsible for ensuring that their use of Caffein complies with applicable privacy and data-protection requirements within their jurisdiction.

If you have concerns about information processed within a specific Discord server, you may also contact the server's administrators.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including the right to:

* Request information about data associated with you;
* Request correction of inaccurate information;
* Request deletion of certain information;
* Object to or restrict certain processing;
* Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
* Submit a privacy-related complaint to an appropriate regulatory authority.

Some requests may be limited where retaining information is necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or legitimate operational purposes.

## 10. Data Deletion Requests

If you want information associated with your Discord account or server to be deleted, you may contact the Caffein maintainers through the project's GitHub repository.

When submitting a request, provide enough information for us to identify the relevant data without unnecessarily providing additional personal information.

We may need to verify the request before processing it.

Some information may be retained when required for security, legal, or other legitimate purposes.

## 11. Children's Privacy

Caffein is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

Users should not provide unnecessary personal or sensitive information through Caffein.

Because Caffein operates through Discord, your use of the Application is also subject to Discord's age requirements and policies.

## 12. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information processed by Caffein.

However, we cannot guarantee that information transmitted through Discord, the Internet, or third-party services will always be completely secure.

Users should avoid submitting passwords, payment information, government identification documents, or other highly sensitive information through Caffein.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to Caffein, its features, data practices, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the **Last Updated** date at the beginning of this document will be updated.

Continued use of Caffein after changes to this Privacy Policy become effective constitutes acknowledgment of the updated policy.

## 14. Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or Caffein's handling of information, please contact the Caffein maintainers through the project's GitHub repository.
